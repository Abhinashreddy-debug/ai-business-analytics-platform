# Data dictionary
- order_id: unique synthetic transaction ID
- order_date: transaction date
- region: sales region
- category/product: product grouping and item
- units_sold: quantity
- unit_price: unit selling price in INR
- discount_pct: discount fraction (0.10 = 10%)
- marketing_spend: allocated marketing spend in INR
- customer_type/payment_method: customer and payment attributes
- revenue/cost/profit: synthetic transaction financial measures in INR

The dataset is synthetic and for learning/demo only.
